Menu and navigation

Decide what appears in the menu, and which roles see it.

What somebody actually sees is the overlap of four things: what the product has, what you enable here, what their role is given, and what their permissions allow. Hiding a menu item tidies the navigation — it does not grant or remove access, which is checked on every request regardless.
